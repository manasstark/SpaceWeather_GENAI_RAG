Is the claim supported by the passages? "supported" means a passage states the same thing or something that clearly implies it. General physics that a passage explains counts as support for the same general statement. A claim that applies a passage's general statement to a specific event is still "supported" if it adds no new facts. A claim that adds a cause, a number, a place, a date or an outcome that no passage gives is "not_supported".

Answer with JSON only: {"verdict": "supported" or "not_supported", "reason": "one short sentence"}
